## IMPLICATIONS (my recommendations / assumptions)
- No builder-provided TV/network point in study/elders' room or kids' room: add to electrical scope (husband WFH: wired LAN to study is a must).
- AAC walls: wall-anchored tall units, heavy wall-mounted units, TV and pooja niche need hollow-block/chemical anchors; a recess niche in an AAC wall needs a lintel/frame detail — confirm with builder or structural advice.
- Kitchen chimney duct must exit via a shaft/façade opening; brochure shows no explicit duct provision — ask builder. Gas provision is in the kitchen.
- Invisible grills must fit over UPVC sliding balcony doors/MS railings; check with the grill vendor and builder (RWA rules).
- 5'-wide balconies: no climbable furniture within 1 m of railing.
