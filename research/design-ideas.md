# Design ideas for Warm Walnut Modern
_Accessed 2026-11-15. Ideas are my synthesis; links are sources (no images copied). Sections marked "mine" cite no firm._

## Inspired by sources
- **Walnut arch as divider:** a designer post says a walnut arch adds warmth, perceived height and room separation (https://www.pinterest.com/pin/jessica-kathryn-on-instagram-we-call-it-the-walnut-arch-this-feature-not-only-elevates-your-space-both-figuratively-and-literally-it-is-full-of-benefits-ot-in-2024--197595502404088566/). → Kitchen–dining arch in a veneer-wrapped frame, rounded edges.
- **Walnut + white + sage tile kitchen:** similar palette in an OUTLINE Homes project (https://in.pinterest.com/pin/1113515076612828178/). → white handleless shutters, walnut peninsula, sage stacked tile.
- **Smoked walnut veneer + natural brass in a Bangalore high-rise:** Studio Prakruthi (https://www.wallpaper.com/design-interiors/interior-design/bangalore-apartment-studio-prakruthi). → use walnut veneer on accent zones only; keep the rest ivory.
- **Heirloom teak, cane, brass as heritage touch:** Vedanth Design Studio (https://www.houzz.in/magazine/bangalore-houzz-this-modern-home-shows-off-its-heirlooms-stsetivw-vs~154707165). → brass/jaali pooja detail, cane stools, handloom throws.
- **Brass + linen + cane palette:** Weespaces Houzz tour (found via search; exact tour URL not captured [U]).

## Mine (assumptions / recommendations)
1. Walnut veneer only where seen (arch, peninsula front, TV panel, pooja frame); walnut-look laminate inside wardrobes. Possible saving ~₹1–1.5L [assumption].
2. Pooja niche ~12"W x 15"H x 9"D, brass jaali back, base ≥ 54" above floor, 2700K LED, off any toilet wall.
3. Master has builder teak-brown wood floor, so keep bedroom furniture lighter (oak/ivory) to avoid a dark, flat look.
4. Kitchen: ducted chimney ≥1200 m³/hr; leave a 20 mm channel/blocking in the peninsula bulkhead for the future sliding glass panel.
5. Lighting: 2700K layers; three brass dome pendants over the peninsula; cove lighting only in living.
6. Toddler: rounded edge profiles, soft-close, magnet locks, wall-anchored tall units.
7. Kids' room: neutral sage/ivory base, swappable accents, conduit and sockets pre-wired on the east/north wall for a future desk.
8. Study/elders' room: wall-mounted fold-down desk plus storage daybed; desk faces north or east.
